Every property owner eventually faces this question: is it time to patch, or is the whole lot due for a repave? The answer usually comes down to how widespread the damage is.

<!--more-->

## When patching makes sense

If potholes and cracking are isolated to a few spots and the rest of the surface is structurally sound, patching is the fast, budget-friendly fix. A well-executed patch can hold up for years, especially when paired with regular sealcoating.

## When it's time to consider repaving

- Cracking covers more than 25-30% of the surface
- Potholes keep reappearing in the same spots after patching
- The base layer beneath the asphalt is visibly failing
- Drainage issues are causing water to pool and erode the surface repeatedly

## Our recommendation

We always inspect before we recommend — a patch job on a failing base is a short-term fix that costs more in the long run. If your lot has more than a few problem spots, it's worth getting an honest assessment before deciding.
